Walk Levels

E - Easy: A walk of up to 4½ hours on good surfaced tracks and less than 200 metres total ascent.

M - Moderate: up to 12km, less than 400 metres total ascent and less than 50% rough going.

MS - Moderately Strenuous: Up to 600 metres total ascent and/or no more than 15 km with some rough going taken at a reasonable pace.

S - Strenuous: More than 600 metres total ascent and/or more than 15km with steep, loose or heavily vegetated sections.

VS - Very Strenuous: More than 800 metres total ascent and/or more than 20km with steep, loose or heavily vegetated sections.

X - Experienced walkers routes: Routes which require considerable stamina and/or significant scrambling skills on rough, steep or exposed sections.

Scr - Scrambling: This implies the need for handholds to ascend steep rock pitches with a sense of exposure to vertigo and where a slip could lead to a serious accident.

Scr0 Very easy scrambling. Short sections requiring the use of handholds on not very steep or sustained sections. No exposure.

Scr1 Easy scrambling, requiring the use of handholds on not very steep or sustained sections. No exposure.

Scr2 Scrambling with some technical moves over steep and more continuous sections. With the probability of some exposure.

Scr3 Technically difficult scrambling. Sometimes over prolonged sections, possibly very exposed and with steep drops to one or both sides. Not easy to reverse down or escape from.

Terrain

A - All good tracks

B - Less than half the route on rough ground

C - More than half the route on rough ground or routes with difficult steep awkward/rocky sections.
